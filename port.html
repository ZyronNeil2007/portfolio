About Me

I'm Zyron Neil A. Bautista, a Computer Science student at Isabela State University - Cabagan Campus, passionate about technology, design, and innovation. I specialize in layout design, web development, and programming, combining creativity with functionality to produce visually appealing and user-friendly outputs.

I have experience in developing projects such as a school website and the BNHS Online Quiz Website, which focuses on improving students' learning through interactive and accessible digital tools. My work reflects my strong interest in creating systems that are not only efficient but also engaging for users.

As a layout artist, I take pride in crafting designs that communicate clearly and effectively. I currently serve as a campus layout artist for "The Gayser" publication and as the Head Layout Artist in the College of Engineering, where I lead visual design efforts and ensure high-quality outputs.

Beyond academics, I enjoy exploring new technologies, watching films and series, reading, and working on creative projects. I am continuously learning and striving to grow as a future software engineer, aiming to build impactful digital solutions.
